System rules — read first, then the boards
Colour discipline. Paper #F6F3EE ground, white sheets, hairline #DFE1E7. Steel Blue = interaction + evidence. Seafoam/Amber/Coral = verdicts only, as washes + deep text, never solid fills. Neon Lime = exactly one primary action per screen (+ the dirty-dot and check-disc accents); the one stated exception is lime-on-Midnight inside the featured pack card (R6-1) — the house CTA-band pairing, allowed alongside the screen's pay button. Midnight fills: the OG card family (R8-2/R8-5), the recommended pack, the top-bar FI pill, the user’s chat bubble (C9), and timeline/check discs — never a page background on paper surfaces.
Register. Every surface is a document: numbered (№), dated, sourced, versioned (engine v2.3). No dashboards, no sidebars, no cards-for-cards’-sake. The seam is honest (severity + cost ranges visible), refusal is a first-class state, and nothing is charged before a verdict exists. Voice: analyst who reads the fine print — “The yield in the listing is not the yield you would own.”
Coverage. Six files, 60+ frames, nothing deferred: core journey (R1–R8), emails ×6 (R9), account (R10), refunds (R11), tracking (R12), compare (R13), notifications (R14), onboarding (R15, on R1), deletion & export (R16), print P1–P4 incl. the bank summary, plus the marketing landing. Every flow has entry, success, error and empty/edge (validation R1-3, fetch-fail R1-9, withdrawn R1-10, refusal R1-11, declined R6-3, zero-credits R6-4, listing-ended R8-3, empty drawer R10-3) and three breakpoints + a Finnish pass per board. Touch targets ≥44 px; focus order and aria-live noted per board.
